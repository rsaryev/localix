# Community Code of Conduct

Localix contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, constructive,
and safe for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their merits.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith while evidence is incomplete.
- Respect project scope, maintainer decisions, and other people's time.
- Protect private information and disclose security-sensitive findings privately.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information or credentials without explicit permission.
- Spam, impersonation, deceptive reports, or knowingly misleading technical claims.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates
these expectations. Depending on severity and history, responses may include a warning, temporary
restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

For an incident in a GitHub-hosted space, use GitHub's report and block tools; serious abuse can
also be reported through [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not
use the vulnerability-reporting channel for a conduct concern: it is reserved for software security
reports. A dedicated private project contact will be added before public community spaces open;
until then, do not put sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people needed to review and resolve the report. Anyone
handling a report must disclose a conflict of interest and step aside when appropriate.
