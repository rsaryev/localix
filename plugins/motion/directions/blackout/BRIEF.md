# Brief

**This film is the Blackout sample.** It's a 14.5 s landscape typography study with no product, logo
or claims. Five statements land on one 12-column grid: a wall of noise, a cleared room, one line on
one rule, a word that gets louder in steps, and a final word blacked out by a bone slab. Every
`data-sample` line is illustrative copy: replace it and remove the attribute.

What to keep when you fork it: the grid and furniture (label, beat index), oversized left-aligned
type in masks, one move per beat with hard holds, hard cuts and two straight wipes, black and bone
only. The noise wall is built from the fragments in `#noise-source` / `#room-source` (keep both
lists identical so the cut at 2.9 s matches). "louder." and "Blackout." are fitted to the grid width
at build time, so any word fits.

Fill this in before rewriting. Every line on screen must trace back to a source below.

- **Audience:** who watches, where (landing hero, social feed, launch post), with or without sound.
- **One message:** the single thing they should remember.
- **Action:** what they should do after (CTA and URL).
- **Format and length:** landscape / portrait / square; 10–40 seconds.
- **Look:** Blackout: black and bone, no accent; oversized type on a measured grid.
- **Sources:** the real copy, logo and numbers and where they come from. No invented claims.
