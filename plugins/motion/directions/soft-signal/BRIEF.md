# Brief

Sample brief for the Soft signal direction. Replace every line with the real brand's sources before
building; the copy on screen is illustrative.

- **Audience:** people meeting the brand in a launch post, a site hero or before a talk. Works
  without sound.
- **One message:** a gentle brand with presence ("Shape a new feeling.").
- **Action:** remember the mark and the name; the end card holds the lockup and tagline.
- **Format and length:** landscape 1920×1080, 10.4 s, three scenes.
- **Look:** deep violet ground, lavender translucent petals in CSS 3D depth (depth of field: far and
  near petals soft, mid petals sharp), light sans type with generous tracking on the line and the
  tagline. Glow only on the mark; one light leak at the climax.
- **Sources:** sample copy only. Replace the petal fan in `compositions/fold.html` and
  `compositions/signoff.html` with the real mark (keep four layers if the logo allows it, or fold
  the planes into an imported `assets/logo.svg`), and the name and tagline in `signoff.html`.
