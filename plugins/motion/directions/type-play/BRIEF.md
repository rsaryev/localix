# Brief

**This film is the Type play sample.** It's a 9.8 s landscape poster in four shouts on a 0.4 s beat
(150 BPM): "Make some noise." with a running ticker, "Move." on an inverted black sheet, "Break the
pattern." over a bar pattern that falls out of step, then "Your next … idea." with a word carousel.
There's no product, logo or claim. Every `data-sample` line is illustrative copy: replace it and
remove the attribute.

What to keep when you fork it: heavy uppercase lines fitted edge to edge across the safe area (they
are measured at build time, so any copy fits), the hot word on an accent slab, hits on the beat or
half beat, one overshoot per scene, and hard cuts plus one push-slide. Carousel words live in
`#idea-slot`: the last one sticks.

Fill this in before rewriting. Every line on screen must trace back to a source below.

- **Audience:** who watches, where (landing hero, social feed, launch post), with or without sound.
- **One message:** the single thing they should remember.
- **Action:** what they should do after (CTA and URL).
- **Format and length:** landscape / portrait / square; 6–30 seconds.
- **Look:** Type play: acid ground, black type, one hot slab colour.
- **Sources:** the real copy, logo and numbers and where they come from. No invented claims.
