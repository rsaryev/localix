# Brief

Sample brief for the Field notes direction. Replace every line with the real sources before
building; the copy on screen is illustrative.

- **Audience:** readers of a studio journal, a newsletter launch or a report: a site hero, a post or
  the start of a talk. Works without sound.
- **One message:** the work moves at a considered pace and every page is accounted for.
- **Action:** read the issue; the closing spread names it (add a URL to the folio line if needed).
- **Format and length:** landscape 1920×1080, 11.6 s, three scenes.
- **Look:** paper, ink and one deep green; serif headlines and body, tracked small-caps labels in a
  quiet sans, rules and folios like a printed page. Measured motion, no glow, light grain.
- **Sources:** sample copy only. Replace the headline and standfirst (`pace.html`), the stations,
  times and document (`handoff.html`, mirrored in `page.html`), and the spread's masthead,
  paragraphs, quote and caption (`page.html`). A real screenshot or scan can replace the card's
  contents; keep the card size, or update the CARD numbers in both scenes together.
