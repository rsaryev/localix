# Brief

Sample brief for the Nightfall direction. Replace every line with the real brand's sources before
building; the copy on screen is illustrative.

- **Audience:** people meeting the brand for the first time — a site hero, a launch post or the
  opening of a presentation. Works without sound.
- **One message:** a brand that is confident without raising its voice ("Quietly bold.").
- **Action:** remember the name; the end card holds the wordmark (add a URL under it if needed).
- **Format and length:** landscape 1920×1080, 16.8 s, five scenes.
- **Look:** warm graphite, a serif display at medium weight, a quiet tracked sans for labels, one
  ember accent used once per scene as the single glowing focal point. Centred, symmetrical, slow.
- **Sources:** sample copy only (an independent brand study). Replace the lines, the brand name and
  the sun mark in `compositions/signature.html` with the real logo (inline SVG paths or an imported
  `assets/logo.svg`).
