# Brief

Sample brief for the Connect direction. Replace every line with the real story; every line on screen
must trace back to a source below.

- **Audience:** people learning a method or a feature (explainer on a docs page or a feed), watching
  without sound.
- **One message:** separate pieces become one clear next step.
- **Action:** take the next step (the yellow outcome pill on the last frame).
- **Format and length:** landscape 1920×1080, 11 s.
- **Look:** saturated cobalt, white cards, one yellow highlight for the key word and the outcome;
  clear sans; diagram logic on a dot grid. Wipes in reading direction only.
- **Sources:** sample copy only ("Find the question. Join the dots. Make the next step.", the three
  items An idea / An insight / A plan and their one-line examples, the outcome pill). Keep three
  items at most per scene; replace the example lines with real ones.
